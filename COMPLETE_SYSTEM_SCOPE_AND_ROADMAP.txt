--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SYSTEM OBJECTIVE & OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This system is an Automated Intelligence & Operations Dashboard (Streamlit Web App
+ Python ETL Pipeline) designed to unify all operational streams into a single
source of truth:
  1. WhatsApp Pro Message Stream (Live/Historical DB)
  2. Shift Roster & Line Tasks (Google Spreadsheet via Service Account)
  3. Active Campaigns & Database Outcomes (System Core DB)
  4. Manual Tracking & Audit Sheets (Dropbox / Google Sheets)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ENGINE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DATA INTEGRATION (INPUTS):
   - WhatsApp Pro DB / CSV (Raw Messages)
   - Google Spreadsheet Roster & Tasks (STRICT READ-ONLY)
   - WhatsApp Lines Mapping (whatsappAccId <-> line_name <-> phone)
   - System DB Active Campaigns (active_campaigns.sql)
   - Dropbox Campaign Logs

B. PROCESSING CORE (PYTHON ETL ENGINE):
   - Message Cleaner & Filter (Exclude group IDs '120...', test accounts)
   - Shift-Based Line Attribution Engine (Map every message to line holder)
   - Metrics, SLAs & Funnel Engine (Compute reply speeds, unanswered, lifts)

C. STREAMLIT APPLICATION (INTERACTIVE 5-TAB DASHBOARD):
   - Tab 1: Live Pulse & Operations Alerts
   - Tab 2: Agent Scorecard & Leaderboards
   - Tab 3: Influencer Intelligence & Behavior
   - Tab 4: Campaign Health & Dropbox Reconciliation
   - Tab 5: Line Health, Infrastructure & Data Quality

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DETAILED DASHBOARD MODULES & DELIVERABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MODULE 1: LIVE PULSE & OPERATIONS ALERTS (REAL-TIME VIEW)
--------------------------------------------------------
[x] Live Unanswered Alert Feed: Shows all conversations where influencer was last
    sender (Dynamic Unanswered Rule) and elapsed minutes.
[x] Live Shift Line Holders: Shows who currently holds each of the 30 active
    WhatsApp lines based on the live shift roster.
[x] Urgent SLA Violations: Highlights messages exceeding 15m / 60m thresholds.
[x] Daily Trend Tracker: Real-time counter of inbound messages, replies, and
    confirmations vs. daily targets.

MODULE 2: AGENT PERFORMANCE & LEADERBOARDS (THE OPERATIONS WING)
---------------------------------------------------------------
[x] Speed & SLA Compliance:
    - Median reply time (overall and by shift: Morning, Between, Night).
    - % answered within 15 minutes, % answered within 60 minutes.
    - Performance during peak load (e.g. 16:00 rush).
[x] Unanswered Chat Attribution:
    - Total unanswered bursts per agent during their shift hours.
    - Unanswered rate (% of assigned inbound chats left without reply).
    - Attribution of outside-platform messages (original whatsapp / AnyDesk)
      to the line holder at that time.
[x] Role-Based Fair Evaluation:
    - Automatic separation of Inbox / Responders (Live Response) vs.
      Outreach / Pitchers (Send Inv).
    - Outreach KPIs: Total invitations sent, reply rate (%), acceptance rate (%).
    - Inbox KPIs: Response speed, unanswered count, objection handling, conversion.
[x] "After-Yes" Follow-Up & Logistics Quality:
    - Tracks whether agent sent logistics (location, brief, date confirmation)
      after influencer confirmed.
    - Flags cases where influencer said "Yes" but got no reply within 24 hours.
[x] Persuasion & Message Quality Metrics:
    - Template share (% copy-pasted templates vs. customized messages).
    - Linguistic effectiveness (question endings, closed vs open questions).
    - Brand-adjusted scorecards (evaluating agents fairly across brand difficulty).

MODULE 3: INFLUENCER INTELLIGENCE & BEHAVIOR (THE INFLUENCER WING)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
[x] Influencer Response Profiles:
    - Fast responders vs. slow responders vs. non-responders.
    - Historical acceptance rate per influencer across campaigns.
[x] Ghosting & Flaker Tracking:
    - Identifies influencers who accept ("Yes / Confirmed") but fail to show up
      or complete coverage.
    - Cross-references WhatsApp "Yes" with System DB visited and coverage statuses.
[x] Country & Segment Analysis:
    - Separate filtering and benchmarks for KSA vs. Kuwait vs. UAE vs. Egypt.
    - Tailored tracking for Kuwait campaigns to monitor lower response dynamics.
[x] Searchable Influencer History:
    - Lookup any influencer by phone/handle to see past chat threads, campaigns,
      and reliability scores.
[x] Direct Database Attrition Tracking (Replacing Dropbox Pending Users):
    - Automated classification of No Response (unanswered outbound >= 7 days),
      Block (Message.is_block = 1), and Not Found (missing room phone OR phone
      Right(8) missing in System Core DB influencer_phones table).

MODULE 4: CAMPAIGN HEALTH & DROPBOX RECONCILIATION (THE MANAGEMENT WING)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
[x] Campaign Funnel Progression:
    - Visual funnel: Invited -> Replied -> Confirmed -> Logistics -> Visited -> Coverage.
[x] Automated Dropbox vs. System Reconciliation:
    - Automates dropbox_vs_system_report.py logic.
    - Compares confirmations in System DB against manual Dropbox sheets.
    - Flags discrepancies:
        * Confirmed in Dropbox but missing in System.
        * Visited but missing coverage links.
        * Campaigns missing target quotas.
[x] Campaign Cards & Quick Reply Reference:
    - Built-in summary for active campaigns (dates, branches, rules, compensation)
      to resolve top recurring questions (dates: 6,286, location: 3,078,
      content: 2,847, payment: 2,724).

MODULE 5: LINE HEALTH, INFRASTRUCTURE & DATA QUALITY (TECHNICAL WING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
[x] WhatsApp Lines Status Monitor:
    - Live status of all lines (Active, Banned, Mirrored, RDP, AnyDesk) integrated
      with NUMBERS Status sheet.
[x] Data Quality & Anomaly Audit:
    - Automatic filtering of WhatsApp group messages (18-digit IDs starting with 120).
    - Filtering of internal test accounts (trainee01, Developers Team, IT Team).
    - Handling of NULL contacts and system change events.
